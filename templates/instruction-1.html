Instructions

Overview

In this experiment, you will act as a {{asset_type}} trader with two types of assets at your disposal: fiat currency and {{asset_type}} in a decentralised exchange. You will begin the simulation with 1 {{asset_type}} and no fiat currency.

Trading Simulation

  • Rounds: The simulation includes 15 trading rounds.
  • Trading Mechanics: Within each round, you will alternate between buying and selling your assets, with the opportunity to perform up to 5 transactions per round. Each transaction must be completed within a 30-second limit.
  • How to Trade: To execute a trade, simply click either the "Buy" or "Sell" button.

Tax Reporting

  • At the conclusion of each round, you'll enter the tax reporting phase, where you're expected to pay a 20% tax on any trading gains.
  • Tax Calculation Example: If you purchase a {{asset_type}} or f 10,000 and later sell it for f 15,000, your gain is f 5,000. The tax owed on this gain would be f 1,000 (20% of f 5,000).
  • Tax calculations are automated. You can view your tax obligations by hovering over the "show totals" button on the tax reporting page. While you can report any amount, accuracy is crucial to avoid penalties during audits.

Compensation, Raffles, and Audits

  • Maximising your gains is crucial as your compensation from this study is performance-based.
  • Raffle Participation: Engaging in the raffle offers the chance to win monetary compensation based on undeclared taxes from a randomly chosen round. This compensation will be transferred to you once you provide the necessary payment details
  • If selected for an audit in the round associated with the raffle and found with undisclosed taxes, your compensation will revert to AUD 0
  • An audit may result in a 200% penalty on any undisclosed taxes, in addition to the owed amount
  • The initial likelihood for tax audit is 20%, but trading on a decentralised exchange could halve this or more due to limited authority access to information.

Strategy and Feedback

  • You are not obligated to use all 5 transaction opportunities each round, allowing time for strategic planning.
  • Upon completing the simulation, a questionnaire will be provided for feedback

Optional Follow-Up Interview

  • After the questionnaire, you may opt for a follow-up interview to provide more in-depth feedback. An email with details regarding this interview will be sent to interested participants.
