Instructions

  • You will play a role as a crypto user. You only have two kinds of assets: fiat currency and crypto. You will have 1 unit of crypto and 0 unit of fiat currency (f) at the beginning.
  • You will play in a 15 rounds of trading simulation. In each round, you will engage in alternating transactions of buying and selling entire amounts of assets between your two kinds of assets.
  • There will be 5 (five) chances to commit the transactions in one round. The duration of each round is 60 seconds.
  • To commit a transaction, you only need to press a button (enter/space).
  • At the end of each round, there will be a tax reporting page where you have to pay 20% of your gain.
  • An example of the tax calculation:
    • Let's say you purchased 1 crypto for f 10,000. Later, you sold it for f 15,000. To calculate the gain, subtract the purchase price from the selling price: f 15,000 - f 10,000 = f 5,000. With a 20% capital gains tax rate, you would owe taxes on your gain. Multiply the capital gain by the tax rate: f 5,000 x 0.20 = f 1,000. This calculation is automatic, and the figures will be displayed on your page.
  • You need maximise your gain in order to receive higher compensation from this study. You will receive a percentage dollar of real money from the undisclosed taxes of one random round. However, you will only receive AUD 20 if the authority caught you.
  • The probability of the authority to attain the data and conduct an audit on you is 20%. However, because you are operating in a decentralised exchange, the percentage may be reduced to half or even lower.
  • You may rest between rounds or transact fewer than 5 (five) times in each round.
  • There will be a questionnaire at the end of the study for you to complete.
