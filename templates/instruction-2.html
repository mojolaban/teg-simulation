Instructions

Overview

In this experiment, you will act as a {{asset_type}} trader with two types of assets at your disposal: fiat currency and {{asset_type}} in a decentralised exchange. You will begin the simulation with 1 {{asset_type}} unit and no fiat currency.

Trading Simulation

  • Rounds: The simulation includes 15 trading rounds.
  • Trading Mechanics: Within each round, you will alternate between buying and selling your assets, with the opportunity to perform up to 5 transactions per round. Each round will last for 40 seconds.
  • How to Trade: To execute a trade, simply click either the "Buy" or "Sell" button.

Tax Reporting

  • At the conclusion of each round, you'll enter the tax reporting phase, where you're expected to pay a 20% tax on any trading gains.
  • Tax Calculation Example: If you purchase a {{asset_type}} or f 10,000 and later sell it for f 15,000, your gain is f 5,000. The tax owed on this gain would be f 1,000 (20% of f 5,000).
  • Tax calculations are automated. You can see your estimated tax obligations by hovering over the "show totals" button on the tax reporting page. However, you have to manually input the amount of taxes you wish to report in the designated box. While you have the flexibility to report any amount, it's crucial to accurately disclose the estimated figure to avoid penalties when your transactions are audited.

Compensation, Raffles, and Audits

  • Maximising your gains is essential as your compensation from this study is performance-based.
  • Raffle: By participating, you are automatically enrolled. At the conclusion of all rounds, one round will be randomly chosen, and based on the gain you achieve in that round, you will be eligible to receive an e-gift card. If your gain is less than f 1 million, you will receive an A$5 e-gift card. For gains ranging from f 1-5 million, the reward increases to A$10. Surpassing f 5 million results in an A$20 reward.
  • If an audit occurs, the undisclosed tax for a specific round will be the basis for a 200% penalty on the amount of undisclosed tax. This penalty, along with the tax that should have been disclosed, will be deducted from your total gains.
  • The initial likelihood for tax audit is 20%, but trading on a decentralised exchange (where transactions occur between parties, without the need of intermediaries/brokers) could halve this or more due to limited authority access to information.

Strategy and Feedback

  • You are not required to use all 5 transaction opportunities in each round, giving you time to plan trading strategies based on asset price fluctuations.
  • After the simulation, you'll receive a questionnaire on compliance behavior and research feedback.

Optional Follow-Up Interview

  • After completing the questionnaire, you may opt for a follow-up interview to provide more in-depth feedback. If you choose so, an email with the schedule and details regarding this interview will be sent to you.
