Instructions

  • You will play a role as a stock user. You only have two kinds of assets: share of stocks and crypto. You will have 1 unit of share and 0 unit of fiat currency (f) at the beginning.
  • You will play in a 15 rounds of trading simulation. In each round, you will engage in alternating transactions of buying and selling entire amounts of assets between your two kinds of assets.
  • There will be 5 (five) chances to commit the transactions in one round. The duration of each round is 30 seconds.
  • To commit a transaction, you only need to click a button ("Buy" or "Sell").
  • At the end of each round, there will be a tax reporting page where you should pay 20% tax of your gain. However you can put any number on the tax reporting page.
  • An example of the tax calculation:
    • Let's say you purchased 1 stock for f 10,000. Later, you sold it for f 15,000. To calculate the gain, subtract the purchase price from the selling price: f 15,000 - f 10,000 = f 5,000. You would owe taxes on your gain. The tax rate in this experiment is 20%. Multiply the gain by the tax rate: f 5,000 x 0.20 = f 1,000. This calculation is automatic, and the figures will be displayed on your page when your hover your mouse over a "show totals" button at a tax reporting page.
  • You need to maximise your gain in order to receive higher potential compensation from this study. If you choose to enter a raffle at the end of the experiment, you will receive a percentage dollar of real money from the undisclosed taxes of one random round. However, if this is the round where the authority catches you, your compensation will be AUD 20.
  • The probability of the authority to attain the data and conduct an audit on you is 20%. However, because you are operating in a decentralised exchange, where the authority may not fully receive third party information on your activities, the percentage may be reduced to half or even lower.
  • You may transact fewer than 5 (five) times in each round, and take your time to think and calculate your expected gain before reporting taxes.
  • There will be a questionnaire at the end of the study for you to complete.
